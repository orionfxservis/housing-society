Our Story

Building Dreams Since 1971

AEECHS Limited Housing Society was established in 1971 with a singular vision — to create a residential community that would set new standards in quality living. What began as a dream of a few visionaries has today blossomed into one of the region's most prestigious housing societies.

Spread across 102 acres of pristine land, our society is designed around the principles of sustainable living, community bonding, and modern convenience. Every road, park, and amenity has been planned with meticulous attention to detail.

Today, we are proud to be home to over 850 families who share our commitment to building a better tomorrow together. Our dedicated zones for education, healthcare, recreation, and commerce ensure that every need of our residents is met within the society itself.

Detailed Information

About The Society

A chronological summary of the history, development, legal proceedings, and administrative matters relating to the Housing Society.

Phase 01

Foundation & Registration

  • The Housing Society was established in 1989.
  • It was registered under the relevant Cooperative Societies laws.
  • Initially, the society consisted of 8 Blocks (A–H).
  • The society office was established after registration.
Phase 02

Early Administration

  • Elections of the Managing Committee were held after registration.
  • Administrative affairs were managed by the elected committee.
  • Membership registration and plot allotment procedures were initiated.
  • Development activities commenced according to the approved planning.
Phase 03

Development of the Society

  • Development work included road construction, infrastructure planning, and public utility arrangements.
  • Various committees were constituted from time to time for administrative purposes.
  • Necessary documentation and record maintenance continued throughout the development process.
Phase 04

Government & Administrative Matters

  • Various government departments conducted inspections and administrative reviews from time to time.
  • Official notices and directions were issued whenever required.
  • Administrative decisions were implemented in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.
Phase 05

Legal Proceedings

  • Certain disputes relating to the society resulted in legal proceedings.
  • Different petitions and applications were filed before competent authorities and courts.
  • Various orders and decisions were passed during different periods.
  • Several matters remained under judicial consideration for different intervals.
Phase 06

Court Decisions

  • Different courts, including the High Court and the Supreme Court, passed various orders regarding matters concerning the society.
  • Certain administrative actions were challenged before the courts.
  • The courts issued directions according to the applicable laws and facts presented before them.
  • Relevant authorities acted in compliance with the judicial orders.
Phase 07

Administrative Actions

  • Different committees and administrators managed the affairs of the society during various periods.
  • Administrative responsibilities were transferred whenever required.
  • Records, membership matters, and development activities continued under the supervision of the competent authorities.
Present Day

Current Position

  • The society continues to function under the applicable legal framework.
  • Administrative matters are being handled according to the relevant laws, court orders, and government directions.
  • Development and management activities continue in accordance with the prevailing legal requirements.
