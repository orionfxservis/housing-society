Cookie Policy

This policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies on our website to enhance your browsing experience.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

Green Valley Housing Society uses cookies to improve your experience on our website. We use cookies to understand how you interact with our site, remember your preferences, and provide you with relevant content.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility.

Analytics Cookies: These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

Preference Cookies: These cookies remember your settings and preferences to enhance your experience on return visits.

4. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can usually find these settings in the "Options" or "Preferences" menu of your browser. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this website.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Cookie Policy, please contact us at info@greenvalleysociety.org.
